ABOUT US

PoseMoment was born from the need to create a simple, fun way to take photos directly in your browser. I wanted to design a digital photobooth that works entirely app-free and is easy for everyone to use — from youth and adults to content creators and event organizers.

How it works is simple:

  • 1. Choose a layout
  • 2. Press Start
  • 3. The countdown begins
  • 4. The camera snaps the photos automatically
  • 5. Your final photos appear in a filmstrip
  • 6. Download your filmstrip or generate a custom GIF
